==========================================================
 AI HEALTH COACH - USER GUIDE
==========================================================
Educational tool only - NOT medical advice. Check with your
doctor before changing diet or exercise, especially with a
medical condition or medication.

START
  Open the coach in Telegram and press Start. It will ask a
  few questions (age, height, weight, goal, diet, allergies,
  medical conditions) and then set your daily calorie and
  nutrient targets.

LOG FOOD - two ways
  1. VOICE : record a voice note ("do roti aur ek katori dal").
     Hindi, English and mixed all work. It shows what it heard.
  2. TEXT  : type "had 2 idli and sambar for breakfast".
  (Photo logging is not available yet.)

OTHER THINGS TO SAY
  - "What should I eat next?" / "Plan my day" / "Swap paneer,
    I'm out of it"
  - "I walked 30 minutes", "drank 500 ml water", "8000 steps",
    "my weight is 68.5"
  - "How am I doing today?"
  - "Undo that" (removes what was just logged)
  - "Remember I dislike bhindi" (it remembers preferences)

COMMANDS
  /checkin on 20:30   daily friendly check-in (/checkin off)
  /privacy            what is stored and who sees it
  /delete_my_data     erase everything about you
  /help

GOOD TO KNOW
  - All nutrition numbers are estimates. Correct the coach if
    you know better.
  - Every personalised recommendation starts with a safety
    notice - please read it.
  - The coach will not suggest crash diets or very low
    calories, and sends you to a doctor/dietitian for complex
    medical situations.
  - Your messages and relevant data are processed by
    the DeepSeek AI service to answer you.
